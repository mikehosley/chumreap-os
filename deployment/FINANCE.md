# Finance — Localization Template

> **Deliberately blank.** Fill this in with a qualified finance professional before any real deployment.

## Who fills this in
A finance professional (CFO, financial advisor, or equivalent) who understands the deployment market.

## Why it matters
The software has no opinion about whether the business works. Pricing, margins, cash flow, and funding are the operator's problem — and the most common reason ventures like this fail has nothing to do with code.

## Checklist (prompts, not advice)
- [ ] Unit economics modeled (revenue per resident vs. cost per resident)
- [ ] Pricing set for each tier (real-time, daily diary, pattern analysis)
- [ ] Cash-flow plan for the first 12 months
- [ ] Funding sources identified and terms documented
- [ ] Break-even point calculated (residents, timeline)
- [ ] Contingency reserve sized (what happens in a bad quarter)
- [ ] Related-party transactions identified and papered (loans, shared costs)
