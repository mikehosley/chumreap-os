# Insurance — Localization Template

> **Deliberately blank.** Fill this in with a qualified insurance broker before any real deployment.

## Who fills this in
An insurance broker licensed in the deployment jurisdiction.

## Why it matters
Things go wrong in physical operations: injuries, property damage, data incidents, professional mistakes. Insurance doesn't prevent them; it decides whether the venture survives them.

## Checklist (prompts, not advice)
- [ ] General liability coverage placed and limits documented
- [ ] Professional liability / errors & omissions considered
- [ ] Property coverage placed (for any premises or equipment)
- [ ] Workers' compensation placed (where required)
- [ ] Cyber / data-breach coverage considered
- [ ] Vendor insurance requirements set (what coverage vendors must carry)
- [ ] Exclusions reviewed and understood (what is NOT covered)
