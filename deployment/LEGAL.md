# Legal — Localization Template

> **Deliberately blank.** Fill this in with qualified legal counsel in your jurisdiction before any real deployment. This is the most important file in this folder.

## Who fills this in
A licensed attorney in the deployment jurisdiction. More than one may be needed (corporate, health regulatory, data privacy).

## Why it matters
The software encodes no law and satisfies none. Everything legal — the entity, the contracts, the consent documents, the liability allocation — lives here, drafted by counsel, for this jurisdiction.

## Checklist (prompts, not advice)
- [ ] Operating entity formed and documented
- [ ] Resident agreement drafted (services, fees, termination, house rules)
- [ ] Consent and authorization documents drafted (feed consent, welfare-escalation consent, revocation process)
- [ ] Capacity/incapacity provisions reviewed (how the documents survive incapacity, under local law)
- [ ] Vendor and contractor agreements drafted
- [ ] Liability allocation documented (operator, vendors, licensed providers)
- [ ] Dispute resolution chosen (jurisdiction, venue, arbitration vs. courts)
- [ ] Marketing claims reviewed (no promised outcomes the venture doesn't control)
