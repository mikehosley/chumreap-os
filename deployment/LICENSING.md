# Licensing — Localization Template

> **Deliberately blank.** Fill this in with the relevant licensing authorities (via qualified local counsel) before any real deployment.

## Who fills this in
The business licensing authority in the jurisdiction, plus any sector regulators — via local counsel.

## Why it matters
Running the operation may require licenses the software knows nothing about. In particular: the line between "coordination" (what the venture does) and "care" (what licensed providers do) is drawn by regulators, not by this codebase.

## Checklist (prompts, not advice)
- [ ] General business license / registration obtained
- [ ] Care-service regulation reviewed: does the coordination role trigger any licensing?
- [ ] Food handling permits identified (if the operation touches food)
- [ ] Transport permits identified (if the operation moves residents or goods)
- [ ] Any sector-specific approvals obtained or confirmed unnecessary
- [ ] License renewal calendar created
