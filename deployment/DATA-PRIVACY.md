# Data Privacy — Localization Template

> **Deliberately blank.** Fill this in with privacy counsel or a data protection officer familiar with the deployment jurisdiction before any real deployment.

## Who fills this in
Privacy counsel or a DPO. Note: this is separate from the LEGAL.md generalist — data law is its own domain.

## Why it matters
The system handles personal data by design: resident identities, activity patterns, family contact details. The tier architecture (green/amber/red) is an engineering posture — it is not, by itself, compliance with any data protection law.

## Checklist (prompts, not advice)
- [ ] Applicable data protection laws identified (national, and any US state laws if serving US families)
- [ ] Lawful basis for processing documented per data category
- [ ] Consent records designed to satisfy local requirements (not just the in-app toggles)
- [ ] Data retention schedule set (what is kept, for how long, when it is deleted)
- [ ] Breach notification obligations identified (who must be told, how fast)
- [ ] Cross-border transfer rules addressed (resident in one country, family in another)
- [ ] Subject rights process defined (access, correction, deletion requests)
- [ ] Subprocessor inventory (every service that touches the data: sheets, email, hosting)
