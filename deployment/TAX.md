# Tax — Localization Template

> **Deliberately blank.** Fill this in with a qualified tax advisor in your jurisdiction before any real deployment.

## Who fills this in
A licensed tax advisor or CPA in the jurisdiction of deployment.

## Why it matters
Operating this system — collecting fees, paying staff and vendors, holding or leasing property — may create tax obligations from day one. The software does not track, calculate, or remit any tax. That is entirely on the operator.

## Checklist (prompts, not advice)
- [ ] Entity structure chosen and its tax treatment documented
- [ ] Income tax obligations identified (national + local)
- [ ] Property / occupancy tax obligations identified
- [ ] Business, gross-receipts, VAT, or GST obligations identified
- [ ] Withholding obligations for staff and contractors identified
- [ ] Taxable events mapped: which money movements trigger which filings
- [ ] Filing calendar created (what, when, where)
- [ ] Record-keeping requirements documented (what records, how long)
