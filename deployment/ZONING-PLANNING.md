# Zoning & Planning — Localization Template

> **Deliberately blank.** Fill this in with the relevant zoning and planning authorities (via qualified local counsel or a land-use consultant) before any real deployment involving physical premises.

## Who fills this in
Local zoning / planning authorities, usually via a land-use attorney or consultant in the jurisdiction.

## Why it matters
Where residents live is regulated: what a building may be used for, how many people may occupy it, what services may operate from it. Software doesn't change land use.

## Checklist (prompts, not advice)
- [ ] Permitted use of each premises confirmed for the intended operation
- [ ] Occupancy limits identified and documented
- [ ] Any change-of-use approvals obtained (or confirmed unnecessary)
- [ ] Building, fire, and accessibility codes identified
- [ ] Signage and advertising rules checked
- [ ] Parking and traffic requirements checked
- [ ] Neighbor / community notification requirements checked (if any)
