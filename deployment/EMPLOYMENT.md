# Employment — Localization Template

> **Deliberately blank.** Fill this in with employment counsel or a qualified HR professional in your jurisdiction before hiring anyone.

## Who fills this in
Employment counsel or an HR professional familiar with local labor law.

## Why it matters
Coordinators, runners, nurses (even contracted), kitchen staff — the moment anyone works for the operation, labor law applies. Misclassifying employees as contractors is one of the most expensive mistakes a young venture can make.

## Checklist (prompts, not advice)
- [ ] Employee vs. contractor classification reviewed for each role
- [ ] Employment contracts drafted (or contractor agreements, correctly classified)
- [ ] Minimum wage, hours, overtime, and leave rules identified
- [ ] Payroll tax and social-security obligations identified
- [ ] Workplace safety obligations identified
- [ ] Background-check rules and limits identified (especially for staff entering residents' homes)
- [ ] Termination rules and required process documented
