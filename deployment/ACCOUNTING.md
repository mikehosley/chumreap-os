# Accounting — Localization Template

> **Deliberately blank.** Fill this in with a qualified accountant in your jurisdiction before any real deployment.

## Who fills this in
An accountant familiar with the deployment jurisdiction's reporting standards.

## Why it matters
Resident fees, vendor payments, staff payroll, and refunds all need books. The software logs *events* (who ordered what, when) — it is not an accounting system and does not produce financial statements.

## Checklist (prompts, not advice)
- [ ] Chart of accounts established for the operating entity
- [ ] Resident billing and receipts process defined
- [ ] Vendor / contractor payment process defined
- [ ] Payroll accounting process defined (or provider named)
- [ ] Refund and dispute accounting handled
- [ ] Applicable accounting standards identified (local GAAP / IFRS / other)
- [ ] Reporting cadence set (monthly close, annual statements)
- [ ] Audit requirements identified (is an external audit required, and when)
